# Why

Why the laplace transform you may ask? As it turns out, I have studied the Fourier Transform before, which was very complicated and interesting and now its time to expand our wings to this new idea.
The motivation to start writting again came from, unsuprisingly, a [3B1B](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown) video.
The wonderful visualisations, compiled together with great knowledge is just so mesmorising to watch.

In addition to the great explanations, the Laplace transform actually has some great uses that link directly to one of the topics I am studying in Further Math (AS/AL-Level CIE 9231).
For example, in solve second order differential equations, which so far I have done by making 'guesses'.
